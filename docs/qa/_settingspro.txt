Reconnecting

When a platform stops receiving your stream, LIVETAP retries that platform only. Your other destinations are never interrupted.

Reconnect automatically

10 attempts, first wait 1 second, longest wait 30 seconds, doubling each time. Editing the policy is not in this build; the defaults are the ones the adapters were tested against.
