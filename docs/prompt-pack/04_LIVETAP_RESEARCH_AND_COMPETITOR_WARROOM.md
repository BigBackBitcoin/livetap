# RESEARCH QUESTIONS

For each competitor:

1. What does it do exceptionally well?
2. What does it make unnecessarily difficult?
3. What do beginners complain about?
4. What do advanced users complain about?
5. What causes setup friction?
6. What causes stream failures?
7. What is confusing?
8. What requires too many clicks?
9. What is hidden?
10. What is impossible?
11. What is expensive?
12. What mobile limitations exist?
13. What multistreaming limitations exist?
14. What account-management problems exist?
15. What support/reliability problems exist?
