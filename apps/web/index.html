Go live everywhere.
Without becoming a broadcast engineer.

LIVETAP is a free, open-source live production app for streamers and creators. Connect YouTube, Twitch, TikTok and more, say what you are making, tap GO LIVE. One production, every platform, each on its own connection.

Try the web demo Runs on your machine. Nothing on this page is broadcast.

Break it yourself.

Drag a live destination off the stage. Its connection snaps, the others never notice, LIVETAP explains what happened and brings it back on its own.

Or drag any live tile off the stage. Keyboard: focus a live tile and press Delete.

One production. Every shape.

16:9 for YouTube and Twitch, 9:16 for TikTok and Instagram, square for clips. Switch the shape and the picture re-frames, with the space the platform keeps for chat drawn on it.

Tap a Moment. The picture changes.

Six looks that come with the production, nothing to build first. Camera, microphone and screen switch on where they are needed.

Every look is the same production, re-composed. Chat sees the switch as a cut, not a reload.

Instead of OBS.

OBS is powerful and it works. It also names fourteen concepts before your first stream and cannot multistream without a plugin. LIVETAP asks two questions.
